✨ What is NaviBeat?

NaviBeat is a companion widget designed exactly for the Omarchy bar (your sleek, customizable status bar). It sits quietly in your bar, ready to show you the soundtrack of your digital life. Whether you are listening to Spotify, a local MPRIS player, or rocking your own Navidrome / OpenSubsonic server, NaviBeat makes that information glanceable — no alt-tabbing required.

Think of it as yout music dashboard. But instead of a separate window, it's right where you look. You get the track name, artist, album, cover art, your personal star rating, and even the exact bitrate and format — straight from your own server.

🚀 Key Features

🔄

Universal MPRIS

Works with any MPRIS-compatible player (Spotify, VLC, Firefox, many more). See current track, play/pause, timeline, and artwork.

⭐

Server-Side Stars & Ratings

Your Navidrome/OpenSubsonic server knows your taste. Now you can star and rate tracks directly from the widget — talk about clever.

🎚️

Real Format && Bitrate

See if you're jamming to FLAC, MP3, or something exotic — ne need to open another app. The truth is in the bitrate.

🖼️

Three Artwork Styles

Choose between a large album art display, a compact thumbnail, or a stylish full-color backdrop — adapt it to your bar.

📱

Follow Another Device

Pair it with another instance — like a laptop on the same server. See what's playing on your other machine from across the room.

🧩

Native Omarchy Plugin

Built as an Omarchy plugin — feels at home in your bar. Configure it once and forget it (until the beats change).

🛠️ Quick Setup Guide

After launching, NaviBeat will appear in your Omarchy bar. Here's how to make it yours:

  • Find the widget. By default it appears on the right side of your bar, but you can drag it anywhere you like.
  • Right-click to configure. There you'll find options for artwork size, layout style, and the "Follow" device chooser (when using your own server).
  • Connect to your server (optional). If you use Navidrome or OpenSubsonic, input your server URL, username, and password easily in the settings panel. The widget will then fetch your stars, ratings, and more.
  • Enjoy the tune. Hover over the widget to see expanded details. Like what you hear? Hit the star icon right there.

No need to edit config files by hand. All adjustments happen in the built-in settings in a friendly window.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is this only for Linux / Hyprland? Why do I see "MPRIS"?
MPRIS is a standard Linux media player interface, but fans of other systems can still use the local player part. plus, the server features are platform-independent.

Q: Do I need a Navidrome server?
No, the widget works perfectly as a simple "Now Playing" for any MPRIS-compliant app. The server connection is additive — for the extra juicy details.

Q: Can I pair multiple displays or multiple Omarchy bars?
Yes, but "Follow another device" specifically lets you mirror ih status from a second machine — nice for those who dock laptops.
